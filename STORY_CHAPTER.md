# Opening chapter: evidence and character knowledge

Implementation reference, October 25, 2026. This document contains author-only
story information. Player-facing writing lives in `opening_chapter.py`.

## Chronology and reveal boundary

1. Luca develops the anonymous steam-logic work in his isolated mountain lab.
   Shannon is his creation and companion. The exact dates and order of artifact
   placement, Grace's expedition and the inventions becoming legendary remain open.
2. Luca removes Shannon's compulsory companionship instruction and erases his
   autobiographical memories without his consent. Technical knowledge and
   personality remain. Luca leaves the lab and settles in Copperside; Shannon
   reboots and follows later. Mely alone knows Luca's secret.
3. Boole and Shannon become friends by choice. Boole wants to find the anonymous
   engineer; Shannon wants to understand his origins without treating his new
   friendship as merely a means to that end. Grace shares the engineering interest
   and has already lost her leg to an old site's defenses.
4. Mosswater provides a receiver socket match, a compatible lens and a tower route.
   The match suggests a connection, not proof of who built Shannon. Its homecoming
   links practical progress with his uncertainty and Luca's confidence in the pair.
5. Boiler Tower provides another socket match, an accumulator and a service plate
   referring to a southern lock exchange. The machinery belongs to a wider system.
   At home, the accumulator is physically installed and its existing production
   benefit is explained. No additional payment, item or benefit is awarded by a scene.
6. Later canal/waterworks/plains discoveries lead to the mountain lab. Only signed
   work and Shannon's blueprints establish Luca's authorship there. The erasure,
   non-consent and emotional aftermath are reserved for that reveal and the finale.

## Delivery and continuity

- Evidence is inferred only from saved discoveries or completed conversations.
  Existing progress is recorded as prior evidence, without inventing its original day.
- Mosswater and tower follow-ups are offered on return, not forced over controls.
  Older saves get the same available follow-ups. Deferred scenes remain available.
- Scene page progress is saved after each advance; closing does not finish a scene.
  Completed scenes can be replayed without awards, stock changes or relationship farming.
- Everyday topics reflect the actual Luca/Mely arc status. The quiet well scene
  requires both Mosswater's homecoming and an existing completed personal chapter.
- The journal is a record and a conversation route, not a circuit solution guide.
  It never exposes beacon positions, gate solutions or unreached destinations.
- Shared ivory/copper/teal panels, existing portraits and code-drawn artifact
  silhouettes preserve the game's presentation. Reading pauses simulation and hazards.

Remaining phase 1–3 work (audio, camera/pacing and richer motion) remains independent
of this delivered opening chapter. The southern settlement is the next world expansion.
